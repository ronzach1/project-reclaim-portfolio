# Product direction

## Mission

Help people take back control over the digital consumption they want to reduce — through measurement, social accountability, achievement, and practical tools.

## Vision

A world where control over attention and digital consumption is a social norm and a source of pride — rather than a world where our time is primarily shaped by systems designed to maximize engagement.

## Principles

1. Reduce harmful consumption, not useful screen time.
2. Measure what actually matters.
3. Make self-control socially rewarding.
4. It should feel good to win.
5. Give users leverage, not just advice.
6. Make privacy explicit and granular.
7. Reward both excellence and progress.
8. Build an identity, not just utility.

## Current MVP learning direction

- Short-form content is the initial wedge.
- Phase A validates reliable measurement first.
- Instagram Reels is first; TikTok and YouTube Shorts follow only when justified.
- Aggregate social-app time may also be measured.
- The prototype is local-first: there is no backend, account, or social system yet.
- Social accountability, status, and achievement are important later hypotheses.
- Build the product prototype, not enterprise infrastructure.

This document records durable intent and boundaries, not live feature status.
